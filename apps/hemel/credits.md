# Hemel rail monitor

Network Rail train-describer observations are relayed by Signal Maps. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. [Signal Maps](https://signalmaps.co.uk/about/) · [Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)

Signal and berth mappings are adapted from EASMData under GPLv3. The package includes the licence in `data/easm-license.txt`. [EASMData](https://github.com/Shwam3/EASMData)

Abbey station reports are supplied by railinfo. Train schedules are retrieved through Open Rail. [railinfo](https://railinfo.uk/developers) · [Open Rail](https://openrail.railcam.uk/)

Station names and three-letter codes come from the Office of Rail and Road, table 6329 (31 March 2026). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. [Station reference](https://dataportal.orr.gov.uk/statistics/infrastructure-and-environment/rail-infrastructure-and-assets/table-6329-station-attributes-for-all-mainline-stations/)
